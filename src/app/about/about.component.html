About SellerPulse

We help marketplace sellers see their real profit — not the number before commission, GST, RTO cost, and shipping quietly ate into it.

Why we built this

Selling across Meesho, Amazon, Flipkart, and Myntra means selling across four different settlement formats, four different fee structures, and four different places a return or RTO can quietly turn a "sale" into a loss. Most sellers end up tracking all of it in scattered spreadsheets — or not tracking it at all, and finding out the real margin only when it's too late to do anything about it.

SellerPulse exists to close that gap: upload your settlement reports and dispatch invoices, and get back a SKU-wise profit and loss statement that accounts for every fee, every return, and every RTO — automatically, across every marketplace you sell on.
